# Maraka rental — frontend design

## Design Context

### Users
Czech event organizers, bands, DJs, and wedding/corporate planners renting professional
sound & lighting gear for events up to 600 people. They browse a ~100-item catalog on
desktop and phone, pick a rental term (dates), add gear to a cart, and submit a reservation
request. They want confidence the gear is pro and reliable, and a fast, obvious path to book.

### Brand Personality
Road-tested, professional, energetic. Backstage/stage-crew confidence — not corporate-soft,
not playful. Three words: **robust, energetic, dependable.**

### Aesthetic Direction
Premium "road-case / backstage" industrial. Warm high-contrast **light** catalog (best for
scanning many products and for gear photos shot on white), near-black ink on warm paper,
with a single warm **amber** signal accent (stage tungsten). Bold grotesque display type.
Keep the working dark toggle. Explicitly NOT: emerald/fuchsia, glassmorphism, blur blobs,
gradient text, purple/cyan "AI" palette, everything-in-a-rounded-card.

Fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque** (display) + **Hanken Grotesk** (body).

### Design Principles
1. Conversion-first: the rental term picker and "add to cart" are always one glance away.
2. Show the hero gear first — flagship, image-having products lead; cables/consumables trail.
3. High contrast, warm neutrals tinted toward amber; the accent is rare and load-bearing.
4. Type does the work — strong size/weight hierarchy, few sizes, generous rhythm, not cards.
